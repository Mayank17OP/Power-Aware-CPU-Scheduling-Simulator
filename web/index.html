UNDER THE HOOD

How it works.

One workload. Three different ways to decide what runs next.

01

FCFS

First Come, First Served runs each job to completion in arrival order. Simple, but a long job can hold up everything behind it.

BASELINE
02

Round Robin

Jobs take turns for a fixed time slice. This helps other jobs start sooner, though switching jobs has a cost.

BASELINE
03

Power-aware

Uses the same job rotation as Round Robin, but chooses a CPU frequency each turn based on your energy weight.

IMPORTANT CONTEXT

A model, not a power meter.

Energy is calculated from your power settings and the time the simulated CPU spends running, idle or switching. Response time measures how long a job waits before its first run. A lower frequency can actually use more total energy if it runs longer. Results compare these specific inputs—not real hardware or a globally optimal schedule.
